Privacy Policy

Video to Frames: Extract Photo for Android (com.videotoframe.dh) · Effective date: 14 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Video to Frames: Extract Photo (the "App"), provided by Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). The App lets you save still frames from videos on your phone, and we built it to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • The App does not request internet access. Your videos and the frames you save never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Videos and photos

When you choose a video, the App makes a temporary copy in its private storage so it can play the video and decode frames. All processing happens on your phone. Frames you save go to your device's gallery, in the Pictures/Video To Frame folder. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. The temporary copy is replaced when you open another video, and Android removes it when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

Location and other video details

Videos can contain details such as the recording date, camera model and GPS location. The App reads these only to show them on the Video Info screen and, if you leave "Keep Date & Location" switched on, to copy them into the frames you save. This information stays on your device. Tapping the map on the Video Info screen opens your own maps app at that place; the App itself does not send the location anywhere.

Permissions

  • Access media location (optional): requested only if you tap "Allow Location Access" on the Video Info screen. Android uses it to let the App read the location stored in videos you open from Files. You can decline and still use every other feature.
  • The App uses Android's system photo picker and file picker, so it only sees the videos you choose. It does not request access to your whole photo library or storage.
  • The App does not use the advertising ID.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes. What happens to it then is governed by the app you pick and that app's privacy policy.

Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. After your first save, the App may show Google Play's built-in "rate this app" prompt. Google Play handles the download, the prompt and any rating you give, under Google's Privacy Policy. We receive no personal information from them.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Security and data retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we hold none on any server. Anything the App stores stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
