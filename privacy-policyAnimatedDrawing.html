Privacy Policy for Wiggly: Animated Drawing

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Wiggly ("the app") is a free drawing app published by Digital Hole. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Wiggly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services and no in-app purchases. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your drawings, imported images, brush presets and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you delete a drawing or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this data may be included in your personal Google backup, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Photos and media

When you choose to import an image, the app uses the Android photo picker, which gives the app access only to the image you select. Exports (GIF, MP4, PNG, ZIP) are saved to your device's shared Pictures, Movies or Download folders, or passed to another app only when you tap Share.

Permissions

Wiggly does not request any dangerous runtime permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
