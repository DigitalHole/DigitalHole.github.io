Privacy Policy

Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile apps published by Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. (“Digital Hole”, “we”, “us”) on Google Play and the App Store. It explains what information our apps handle, why, and the choices you have. It covers every app we publish now and in the future.

The short version
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location through our apps.
  • Your drawings, photos, projects, and progress stay on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • Our apps for children have no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and they do not collect personal information.
  • Some of our general-audience apps show ads from Google AdMob. Those apps ask for your consent where the law requires it, and their ad data is handled by Google as described below.
  • We never sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We design our apps to work without collecting personal information. We do not run our own servers for our apps, and we do not receive your drawings, photos, recordings, or usage history.

When you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Google Play and the App Store may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as the number of installs, crash reports, and device types) under their own privacy policies. These statistics do not identify you.

2. Information stored on your device

Our apps save settings and content locally so you can continue where you left off. This includes preferences (such as sound on/off or the selected age group), game progress, and the drawings or projects you create. This data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

3. Device permissions

An app only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and you can refuse or withdraw it in your device settings at any time.

  • Photos and media: used to pick a photo from your device to edit or draw on, or to save your finished artwork to your gallery. Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Microphone: used only for optional voice features (for example, saying a word instead of typing it). Speech is turned into text by your device’s built-in speech recognition service, whose own privacy policy applies. We do not record, store, or send any audio.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback while playing.

4. Advertising (general-audience apps only)

Some of our apps that are not designed for children show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, in line with Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app shows a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the app’s privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Our apps for children never show ads.

5. Children’s privacy

Several of our apps are made for children and take part in Google Play’s Designed for Families program. In these apps:

  • we do not collect, use, or share personal information from children;
  • there are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no in-app purchases, and no chat or social features;
  • links that leave the app (such as rating the app, contacting us, or sharing) are behind a parental gate so that only a grown-up can open them.

These apps are designed to comply with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including its rules for children), and Google Play’s Families Policy. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties, except for the advertising data handled directly by Google in our ad-supported apps (section 4), or where the law requires it.

7. Data retention and security

Because your content stays on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, and to withdraw consent. Since our apps do not collect personal information, there is usually nothing for us to provide. If you have contacted us by email and want that message deleted, or have any other request, write to us using the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps change. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If a change significantly affects how an app handles information, we will also mention it in the app’s release notes.
