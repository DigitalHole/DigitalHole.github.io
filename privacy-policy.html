Privacy Policy

App: Cozy Coloring: Calm & Relaxing (com.digitalhole.cozycoloring)
Developer: Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd.
Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

Cozy Coloring does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app works offline and has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage data or any other personal data. The app does not request internet access.

Data kept on your device

Your colored pages, custom color palettes and app preferences (for example your chosen tap sound) are stored locally on your device so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Photos and your gallery

  • Trace Over Image: when you choose a photo, you pick it using Android's system photo picker. The app only receives the photo you select, uses it on your device to draw over, and does not upload it anywhere.
  • Saving artwork: when you tap save, the image is written to the Pictures/Cozy Coloring folder on your device. On Android 9 and below the app may ask for storage permission for this purpose only.

Third-party services

The app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. This is provided by Google Play and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive any personal information from it.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
