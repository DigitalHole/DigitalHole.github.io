This privacy policy explains how Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Android app Kids Colouring & Drawing Games (package name com.kidscolouring.dh), published on Google Play by Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. (the "App").

The App is a colouring book for young children. It is completely free, contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no sign-in, and works fully offline.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.
  • The App does not request internet access, so no data leaves your device through the App.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Everything the App saves stays on your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The App does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any device identifier, including the Advertising ID.

3. Information stored only on your device

To work properly, the App saves the following data locally in its private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Colouring progress: the pictures your child has coloured, so they can continue later.
  • A parental-gate setting: a simple yes/no flag recording that a grown-up has passed the "Parents only" check (see section 5).

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

4. Saving pictures to the photo gallery

If a grown-up chooses to save a finished picture, the App writes the image to the device's photo gallery (in the "Pictures/Colouring for kids" folder). On Android 9 and older this uses the storage permission only for saving these images. The App does not read, scan or upload any other photos.

5. Parental gate

Before the first save to the photo gallery, the App shows a "Parents only" screen that asks a grown-up to enter their birth year. The year is checked on the device only to confirm the user is 18 or over. It is not stored and not transmitted; only the yes/no result described in section 3 is kept.

6. Permissions

  • Vibrate: for small haptic feedback during play.
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and older only): to save finished pictures to the gallery when a grown-up chooses to.

The App does not use the Internet permission.

7. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal information with third parties.

8. Third-party services

The App does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, social-media or tracking services. It only uses standard open-source Android libraries (AndroidX, Material Components and Lottie for animations), which run entirely on the device and do not collect data.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps; see Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help.

10. Data security and retention

All App data stays in the App's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

11. Your rights

As we do not hold any personal data about you or your child, there is no data for us to access, correct, export or erase. You stay in full control of the data on your device, as described in section 3.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a future version of the App adds a new feature, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes on Google Play.
