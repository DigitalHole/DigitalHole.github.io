Lil Artist – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Lil Artist (“the app”) is a creative and learning app for children aged 2 to 8, published by Digital Hole Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”). We built Lil Artist so children can play safely. This policy explains how the app handles information.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children or anyone else.
  • The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no accounts, and no sign-up.
  • The app does not need an internet connection, and it does not send data to us or to third parties.

Information stored on the device

The app saves a few settings and progress (for example, the selected age group, music on/off, and game progress) only on the device itself. This information never leaves the device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Microphone

In Letter Tracing, the optional “Add your own word” feature lets a child say a word instead of typing it. The app asks for microphone permission only when this feature is used. Speech is converted to text by the device’s built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google’s speech services on most Android devices), and that service’s own privacy policy applies. Lil Artist does not record, store, or transmit any audio, and it uses only the recognised word to show it on screen.

Text-to-speech

Spoken prompts use the device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. No personal information is involved.

Links and parental gate

Links that leave the app (rating the app, contacting us, or sharing a story) are behind a parental gate so that only a grown-up can open them.

Children’s privacy

Lil Artist is designed for children and complies with Google Play’s Families Policy, the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal information to review, correct, or delete.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the date above.
